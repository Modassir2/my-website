I'm Modassir. I come from a Physics, Chemistry, Maths and Computer Science background, which is probably why I keep taking problems apart instead of walking around them.

My first real target was Jarvis from Iron Man. I wanted an assistant that could actually use my computer: see the screen, read files, search the web, remember what mattered. That project became Infinity, and it is still growing.

Along the way I got pulled into local models: llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio, OpenAI-compatible endpoints, prompt engineering. I trained vision models for hand detection, face recognition and screen UI objects. I also fine-tuned a small Gemma 3 1B model on a 4 GB GPU using Unsloth's memory-optimized training to mimic my WhatsApp style. Equal parts research and vanity.

Field note

If it sounds slightly fictional, that is usually the sign I want to try it.

I wrote a small neural network in plain Python and trained it on MNIST, mostly to make sure I understood the maths instead of importing it. Lately I spend more time on networking and self-hosting: Pi-hole, SearXNG, Tailscale, Playit.gg, a Minecraft server, a Flask app or two. Arch inside WSL has been the hard, useful part.

Off the keyboard, I run and cycle. The 10K and the 100 km ride started as a way to stay healthy. They turned into another kind of debugging.
